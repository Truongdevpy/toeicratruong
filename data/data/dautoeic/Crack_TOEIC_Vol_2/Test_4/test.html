Ashvale Airport to Add Quiet Rest Lounges

By Nadia Krause, Transit Monthly

Ashvale International Airport has unveiled a plan to significantly improve the experience of transit passengers. Recognizing that waits of two hours or more are common among passengers with connecting flights, who account for roughly 35 percent of daily traffic, the airport management has decided to add more food counters and to install sound-insulated rest booths in Terminal 3.

The refurbishment project consists of two main stages. Stage 1 involves the rebuilding of the Market Hall to offer better food choices and is scheduled to be finished by 14 October. Once the food area is fully functional, Stage 2 will begin, converting the waiting area beside Gate 40 into the new Quiet Zone. Management expects the whole project to be complete before the winter travel season.

NOTICE: Refurbishment Update

Posted on 14 October

We would like to update passengers and staff on the progress of the ongoing facility renovation at Ashvale International Airport. Please be advised that the Market Hall is scheduled to reopen in three weeks.

In addition, work on the Quiet Zone will start next week. Please note that the general waiting area beside Gate 40 will be closed off. The Ashvale Club Lounge, on the upper floor of Terminal 3, will however stay open and accessible to all eligible passengers while the work goes on.
